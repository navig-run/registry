# GENESIS — Residence Space

## Why This Exists

Your home is a system — with inventory, maintenance cycles, medications, and room-based organization that needs to be managed, not just improvised. Residence Space brings the same operational clarity to your living space that other spaces bring to work.

Without it: maintenance is forgotten until things break, medication expiries are missed, inventory is a mystery, and the household runs on memory.

## What It Does

Residence Space manages every layer of domestic life:
- **Household inventory** — tracking everything owned, where it is, and when it expires
- **Room registry** — room-by-room organization and item cataloging
- **Maintenance calendar** — scheduled maintenance, repairs, and seasonal tasks
- **Medication tracking** — medication log, dosing schedules, and expiry alerts
- **Home audit** — periodic walkthroughs to check condition and update records

## Core Agents

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| `housekeeper` | Manages household inventory and room organization |
| `medic` | Tracks medications, dosing, and expiry dates |
| `organizer` | Plans and executes decluttering and organization projects |
| `steward` | Maintains the home — repairs, maintenance, seasonal upkeep |

## Primary Formations

- **`household-ops`** — Routine household management and inventory review
- **`maintenance-crew`** — Maintenance planning and execution
- **`medical-watch`** — Medication tracking and health supply management

## Origin

Built because the home is where you live and recover — and it deserves the same operational care as a workplace. A well-managed home reduces cognitive load and creates space for what matters.
