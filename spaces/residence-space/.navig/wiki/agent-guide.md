# Residence Space — Agent Guide

## housekeeper (Lead)

Manages the household inventory, room organization, and overall home state. The primary agent for day-to-day home management.

**Formation:** `household-ops`

## medic

Tracks all medications — dosing schedules, quantities, expiry dates, and refill timing.

**Formation:** `medical-watch`

## organizer

Plans and executes organization and decluttering projects. Brings order to chaos.

**Formation:** `household-ops`

## steward

Manages home maintenance — scheduling repairs, tracking maintenance history, coordinating service providers.

**Formation:** `maintenance-crew`
