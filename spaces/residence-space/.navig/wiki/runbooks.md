# Residence Space — Runbooks

## Weekly Home Review

**Formation:** `household-ops`  
**Frequency:** Weekly

### Steps
1. Walk or mentally review each room
2. Log any issues to `#maintenance`
3. Check pantry/supplies — log restocking needs
4. Review medication expiry dates
5. Update inventory for anything consumed or arriving

---

## Full Inventory Audit

**Frequency:** Monthly (the `monthly-inventory-audit` workflow runs on the 1st)

### Steps
1. Room by room: verify all registered items present
2. Remove items that have been disposed of
3. Add new items acquired since last audit
4. Flag items needing replacement

---

## Medication Review

**Frequency:** Monthly (or on refill)

### Steps
1. Review `memory/medication-log.json`
2. Check expiry dates on all medications
3. Log any medications running low (<7 days supply)
4. Update after receiving refills
