# GENESIS — Deploy Space

## Why This Exists

Every software team deploys constantly — and loses track of what changed, when, and why. Deploy Space is the unified deployment log: every release, every diff, every rollback, in one searchable view.

Without it: "what changed in this deploy?" becomes an archaeology project. With it: one command shows you the full story of any release.

## What It Does

Deploy Space tracks the full lifecycle of every deployment:
- **Deploy log** — every deployment with timestamp, version, author, and status
- **Diff view** — what changed between this deploy and the last
- **Rollback tracking** — when things went wrong and what was reverted to
- **Deployment health** — success rate, MTTR, and frequency over time

## Origin

Built because deployment visibility is consistently the #1 gap in engineering teams. When something breaks, the first question is always "what did we deploy recently?"
