# Capability Forge

> Build, test, and publish new tool capabilities for the Navig ecosystem.

**Tier:** studio · **Status:** planned  
**Owner:** navig-run · **Created:** 2026-03-17

---

## Purpose

A capability is a unit of what Navig can *do* — a tool, adapter, or pack that
slots into the registry and becomes available across all spaces. This space is
where capabilities are designed, built in isolation, tested against real
commands, and published to the community pack index.

Every widget in every other space traces back to a capability. This is where
capabilities are born.

---

## What lives here

- Capability specs and design docs
- Draft tool/adapter implementations
- Test suites for new capabilities
- Published pack manifests

---

`tools` · `storage` · `agent`
