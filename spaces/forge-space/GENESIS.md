# GENESIS — Forge Space

## Why This Exists

NAVIG's power comes from its tools and packages. Forge Space is where new capabilities are built — from idea to tested pack, ready to ship to the community.

Describe what you want in plain English. Forge compiles it into a deployable package.

## What It Does

- **Dream compiler** — describe a capability in plain English, get scaffold + implementation
- **Pack testing** — test new packs in isolation before distribution
- **Capability registry** — track every tool and pack in development
- **Ship pipeline** — package, version, and publish to the community store

## Origin

Built so contributors can build new NAVIG capabilities without needing to understand the full internals — just describe what you want and Forge handles the scaffolding.
