# GENESIS — Bureau Space

## Why This Exists

French bureaucracy is its own universe — with its own timelines, its own documents, and its own consequences for missing a deadline. Bureau Space is the administrative operating layer: every official document, every deadline, every dossier, organized and tracked.

This space is for French residents navigating the administrative system: taxes, housing, health coverage, civil status, immigration documents, official correspondence.

Without it: deadlines missed, documents lost, processes half-completed. With it: every administrative thread tracked from start to finish.

## What It Does

- **Document vault** — identity and health documents encrypted in `navig cabinet`
  (searchable by content, expiry alerts, portable backup); letters filed by `navig paperwork`
- **Deadline tracker** — never miss a submission, renewal, or declaration
- **Dossier management** — track each administrative process from start to resolved
- **Correspondence log** — every letter sent and received, with reference numbers
- **Annual calendar** — all recurring bureaucratic obligations

## Origin

Built for people who deal with the French administrative system and want to replace anxiety with organization.
