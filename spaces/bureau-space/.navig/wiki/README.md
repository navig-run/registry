## Document Categories

- `impots/` — tax documents (avis, confirmations, receipts)
- `logement/` — lease, diagnostics, receipts
- `sante/` — Carte Vitale, attestations, remboursements
- `etat-civil/` — identity, family documents
- `immigration/` — séjour, visa, authorization
- `courrier/` — official correspondence
