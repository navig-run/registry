# Bureau Space — Context

French administrative document management. Covers taxes, housing, health insurance (Sécurité Sociale, mutuelle), civil status, immigration, and official correspondence.

## Administrative Categories
- **Impôts**: tax declarations, notices, payment receipts
- **Logement**: lease, diagnostics, utilities, charges
- **Santé**: Carte Vitale, attestations, remboursements, mutuelle
- **État civil**: identity documents, family records, certificates
- **Immigration**: titres de séjour, visas, naturalisation
- **Courrier officiel**: registered letters, official responses
