# Etsy Selling

Templates, tag guides, and SOPs for Etsy listing management.

## Quick Start

1. Research competitors with EtsyRank or Marmalead for keyword data
2. Use 13 tags: mix exact match, broad, and long-tail
3. Lead your title with your strongest keyword
4. Write a story-driven description — Etsy buyers are emotionally driven

## Templates

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `listing-template.md` | Etsy listing copy template with tag guide |

## Etsy SEO Quick Rules

- Title: keyword-first, separated by commas (Etsy reads pipe as separator)
- Tags: 13 tags, max 20 chars each, use all 13
- Categories: be as specific as possible
- Photos: first photo is the most important — use lifestyle image with natural light
