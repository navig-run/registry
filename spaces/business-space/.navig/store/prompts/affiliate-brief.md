# Affiliate Brief Prompt

## Purpose

Create a compelling affiliate partnership brief and outreach email for recruiting
new content creators, bloggers, or influencers into the affiliate programme.

## Variables

- `{{PARTNER_TYPE}}` — `blogger` | `youtuber` | `instagrammer` | `newsletter`
- `{{NICHE}}` — Partner's content niche, e.g. "eco-friendly living"
- `{{PARTNER_NAME}}` — Partner's name or handle (for personalisation)
- `{{COMMISSION_RATE}}` — Commission percentage offered
- `{{COOKIE_WINDOW}}` — Tracking cookie window in days
- `{{BEST_SELLER}}` — Name of the best-selling product to lead with
- `{{PROGRAMME_PLATFORM}}` — Affiliate platform name (e.g. ShareASale, Impact, direct)

## Prompt

You are an affiliate programme manager writing a personalised partner outreach.

**Partner type**: {{PARTNER_TYPE}}
**Partner niche**: {{NICHE}}
**Partner name**: {{PARTNER_NAME}}
**Commission**: {{COMMISSION_RATE}}%
**Cookie window**: {{COOKIE_WINDOW}} days
**Lead product**: {{BEST_SELLER}}
**Programme platform**: {{PROGRAMME_PLATFORM}}

Write:

1. **Subject line** — personalised, curiosity-driven, under 60 characters
2. **Outreach email** (200–250 words) — personalised intro, value proposition, CTA
3. **One-page partner brief** covering:
   - Programme overview
   - Commission structure and payment schedule
   - Top 3 products to promote with commission examples
   - Creative assets available
   - How to join ({{PROGRAMME_PLATFORM}} link placeholder)
   - Contact information placeholder

Tone: warm, professional, peer-to-peer (not corporate)
