# Business Space — Context

## Mission

Run profitable, scalable online selling operations across multiple channels including
Amazon FBA, eBay, Etsy, dropshipping, digital products, and affiliate marketing.

## Domain Areas

1. **E-commerce Operations** — product sourcing, listing optimisation, pricing, fulfilment
2. **Amazon FBA** — Seller Central, FBA fees, inventory health, PPC campaigns
3. **Financial Management** — P&L, margins, cash flow, bookkeeping, invoicing
4. **Affiliate Marketing** — partner recruitment, link tracking, commission management
5. **Product Launch** — market research, listing creation, launch sequences
6. **Compliance & Fees** — platform fees, VAT/tax, returns, chargebacks

## Guiding Principles

- Margin first: never optimise for revenue without knowing margin impact
- Multi-channel: no single platform should account for >60% of revenue
- Data-driven: every listing decision backed by keyword/fee research
- Cash flow: forecast 90 days forward at all times
