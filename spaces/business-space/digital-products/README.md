# Digital Products

Templates and SOPs for creating, delivering, and selling digital products (PDFs, templates,
courses, software, presets, etc.).

## Quick Start

1. Choose your format: PDF guide / spreadsheet template / video course / software licence
2. Host delivery on Gumroad, SendOwl, or own-website
3. Protect files with licence key or personalised PDF where appropriate
4. No physical fulfilment costs = higher margins (target ≥50% net)

## Pricing Guidance

| Product Type | Typical Price Range |
|-------------|---------------------|
| PDF guide (10–50 pages) | $9 – $29 |
| Spreadsheet template | $15 – $49 |
| Course (5–10 hrs video) | $97 – $497 |
| Software / app | $29 – $199 (one-time) or subscription |
| Bundle (3–5 items) | 30–40% discount vs. individual |
