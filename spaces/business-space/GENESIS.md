# GENESIS — Business Space

## Why This Exists

Every e-commerce operation needs a single brain — one place where listings are tracked, margins are calculated, launches are planned, and affiliates are managed. Business Space is that brain.

Without it: scattered spreadsheets, missed restocks, untracked affiliate income, and financial blind spots.

## What It Does

Business Space manages the full lifecycle of an online business:
- **Product operations** — FBA listings, dropshipping, Etsy/eBay selling, product launch checklists
- **Financial control** — P&L reviews, fee tracking, margin calculation, invoice generation
- **Affiliate programs** — partner tracking, commission management, affiliate briefs
- **Growth** — launch pipelines, product research, platform expansion

## Core Agents

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| `listing-optimizer` | Audits and improves product listings |
| `finance-analyst` | Tracks P&L, fees, margins |
| `fulfillment-ops` | Manages FBA inventory and logistics |
| `growth-hacker` | Identifies expansion opportunities |
| `affiliate-manager` | Tracks partners and commissions |

## Primary Formations

- **`ecommerce-ops`** — Daily product + fulfillment operations
- **`financial-review`** — Monthly P&L and margin review
- **`launch-pipeline`** — New product launch coordination

## Origin

Created to bring order to the chaos of running multiple selling channels simultaneously. Built for operators who need their business to run itself.
