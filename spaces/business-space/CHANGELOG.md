# Changelog

All notable changes to business-space are documented here.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-03-11

### Added

**Agents (5)**
- `growth-hacker` — market research, launch planning, channel growth
- `fulfillment-ops` — FBA inventory, replenishment, shipping
- `finance-analyst` — P&L, margins, invoicing, fee tracking
- `listing-optimizer` — multi-channel listing creation and SEO audit
- `affiliate-manager` — partner recruitment, commission tracking

**Formations (3)**
- `ecommerce-ops` — day-to-day channel operations
- `financial-review` — monthly P&L and fee reconciliation
- `launch-pipeline` — end-to-end product launch

**Skills (5)**
- `calculate-margins` — landed cost and net margin per SKU
- `audit-listings` — listing health check with CSV action list
- `generate-invoice` — Markdown invoice written to `finance-ops/invoices/`
- `check-fees` — platform fee snapshot and delta report
- `track-affiliate` — affiliate click/conversion/commission report

**Workflows (3)**
- `monthly-finance-review` — scheduled P&L close (2nd of each month)
- `listing-audit` — weekly listing health check (Mon 09:00 UTC)
- `product-launch` — on-demand full launch with human approval gate

**Prompts (5)**
- `product-listing` — platform-optimised listing copy
- `fba-audit` — structured FBA inventory audit report
- `p-and-l-review` — monthly P&L commentary with recommendations
- `affiliate-brief` — personalised partner outreach and brief
- `launch-checklist` — 4-phase launch checklist (T-60 to T+30)

**Sub-workspaces (8)**
- `ecommerce-web/`, `amazon-fba/`, `ebay-selling/`, `etsy-selling/`
- `dropshipping/`, `digital-products/`, `affiliate-marketing/`, `finance-ops/`
