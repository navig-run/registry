# Freelance Space — Agent Guide

## account-manager (Lead)

Manages client relationships and project communication. Owns the client record and tracks satisfaction and project health.

**Formation:** `client-ops`

## billing-officer

Creates, sends, and tracks invoices. Monitors payment status and escalates overdue accounts.

**Formation:** `billing-cycle`

## timekeeper

Logs billable hours, generates time reports, and monitors against project budgets.

**Formation:** `client-ops`

## strategist

Reviews the freelance business at a strategic level — rate positioning, client mix, capacity, and growth.

**Formation:** `project-scope`
