# GENESIS — Freelance Space

## Why This Exists

Running a freelance business is running a business — with clients to manage, time to track, invoices to send, and rates to protect. Freelance Space is the operational backbone for independent operators.

Without it: invoices go out late, hours are tracked in scattered notes, client context is lost between projects, and the business bleeds time and money.

## What It Does

Freelance Space manages the full independent business lifecycle:
- **Client management** — contacts, relationships, project history, and communication log
- **Time tracking** — logging hours per project and client, generating time reports
- **Invoicing** — creating, sending, and tracking invoices and payment status
- **Project scoping** — defining deliverables, timelines, and rates before work begins
- **Revenue visibility** — income by client, project, and time period

## Core Agents

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| `account-manager` | Manages client relationships and project communication |
| `billing-officer` | Creates invoices, tracks payments, chases overdue |
| `timekeeper` | Logs hours and generates time reports |
| `strategist` | Reviews pricing, client mix, and business direction |

## Primary Formations

- **`client-ops`** — Active client and project management
- **`billing-cycle`** — Monthly invoicing and payment tracking
- **`project-scope`** — New project definition and rate negotiation

## Origin

Built for freelancers who want their work tracked and their business managed — not just improvised.
