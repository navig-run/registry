# GENESIS — Singularity Space

## Why This Exists

We are living through the most consequential civilizational transition in human history. Singularity Space is the long-horizon observatory — a place to track AI frontiers, monitor civilizational signals, version the self over time, and maintain clarity about where you are going.

Without it: important signals are missed, self-evolution is unmeasured, and the future arrives without preparation.

## What It Does

Singularity Space operates on geologic time:
- **Self-versioning** — snapshotting your current state (beliefs, skills, goals, context) at regular intervals to track evolution
- **AI frontier tracking** — monitoring major AI developments, breakthroughs, and trajectory
- **Civilization signals** — logging significant geopolitical, scientific, and cultural events
- **Destiny log** — maintaining a rolling log of intentions, pivots, and future-state visions
- **Insight tagging** — capturing and tagging cross-domain insights for long-term synthesis
