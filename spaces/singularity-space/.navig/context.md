# Singularity Space

The long-arc space. This is where the operator tracks their own evolution, monitors civilization-scale signals, and maintains continuity of self across time.

## Purpose

Singularity space manages four domains:

- **Self versioning** — periodic snapshots of the operator's current beliefs, capabilities, values, and state. A personal changelog for consciousness.
- **Civilization signals** — tracked developments in AI, governance, energy, biology, and other civilization-scale vectors that the operator has decided to monitor.
- **Destiny log** — future-state visions, trajectories, and probability assessments. A living document of where the operator is heading.
- **AI journal** — raw observations, experiments, and insights from working with AI systems. The running log of the AI-human interface.

## Operating Principles

1. **The self is a system.** Systems have versions. Beliefs change. Capabilities evolve. Track it.
2. **Signals without context are noise.** Every civilization signal is rated for strength and relevance to the operator's trajectory.
3. **Destiny is a probability space.** Futures are not predicted — they are probability-weighted and scenario-planned. The log shows the work.
4. **AI is the primary civilizational variable.** The AI journal is the most time-sensitive of the four tracks.
5. **Archive, don't delete.** Old self-versions are primary data. They show the delta. Never remove them.

## Agents

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| `oracle` | Long-horizon synthesis, self-version management, destiny synthesis |
| `signal-tracker` | Civilization signal capture, rating, pattern detection |
| `destiny-keeper` | Destiny log management, trajectory updates, probability notes |
| `ai-researcher` | AI developments tracking, frontier observation, experiment logging |

## Tone

Quiet, long-arc. No urgency. Events processed with geological patience. The signal matters more than the reaction.
