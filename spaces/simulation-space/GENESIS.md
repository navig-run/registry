# GENESIS — Simulation Space

## Why This Exists

You are a system. Your habits are functions. Your decisions are probabilistic outputs. Simulation Space treats the self as a complex system to be understood, debugged, and improved through experimentation.

Without it: habit failures are mysteries, behavioral patterns go unexamined, and self-improvement is just intention without data.

## What It Does

Simulation Space runs the operating system of the self:
- **Agent modeling** — predictive behavior models of the people around you, recalibrated by observation
- **Habit simulation** — tracking habits as system processes with inputs, outputs, and run states
- **Probability tracking** — maintaining probability trees for decisions and outcomes
- **Debug loops** — identifying bugs in behavior, tracing root causes, running fixes
- **Experiment logs** — structured behavioral experiments with hypotheses and results

## Core Agents

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| `habit-engine` | Runs and tracks habits as system processes |
| `debugger` | Diagnoses behavioral bugs and failure modes |
| `agent-modeler` | Models people in your environment and scores its own predictions |
| `probability-analyst` | Maintains probability trees for key decision domains |

## Primary Formations

- **`habit-sim`** — Active habit simulation and tracking
- **`debug-loop`** — Behavioral debug and root-cause analysis
- **`agent-lab`** — Behavior modeling of the people around you

## Origin

Built for operators who apply systems thinking to their own psychology — not to dehumanize themselves, but to finally understand the machine well enough to change it.
