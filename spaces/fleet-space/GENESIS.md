# GENESIS — Fleet Space

## Why This Exists

Some problems are too big for one agent. Fleet Space lets you spawn a swarm of agents, coordinate them on a parallel task, monitor their progress, and integrate their outputs into a single result.

Without it: you run agents sequentially and manually. With it: 7 agents tackle 7 sub-problems simultaneously, and the fleet commander integrates the results.

## What It Does

Fleet Space manages multi-agent parallel execution:
- **Fleet spawning** — launch N agents with distinct sub-tasks from a master prompt
- **Coordination** — fleet commander routes tasks, resolves conflicts, and merges results
- **Progress monitoring** — real-time view of each agent's status and output
- **Result integration** — combine partial outputs into a coherent final result

## Origin

Built for operators who have hit the single-agent ceiling. When the problem is too wide for one thread, you need a fleet.
