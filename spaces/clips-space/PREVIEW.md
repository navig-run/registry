# Clips Space — Preview

> **Short-Form Video Factory** for TikTok, Reels & YouTube Shorts

## What It Does

Clips Space is a complete AI-powered workspace for short-form video creators. It provides agents, workflows, and skills to produce, optimise, and distribute clips across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

## Key Features

- 🎬 **Clip Factory** — End-to-end short-form video production pipeline
- 🔥 **Trend Riding** — Rapid response to trending sounds and formats
- ♻️ **Repurpose Lab** — Convert long-form content into platform-native shorts
- 📅 **Batch Scheduling** — Plan and schedule a week of clips in one session
- 📊 **Cross-Platform Analytics** — Unified performance tracking

## Use Cases

- Create 15-60 second clips optimised for each platform
- Ride trending sounds/formats within hours of going viral
- Repurpose YouTube videos into TikTok, Reels, and Shorts
- Batch produce and schedule a week of content
- Track performance across all platforms in one dashboard

## Agents

5 specialised agents: Clip Director, Hook Writer, Clip Editor, Trend Scout, Distributor

## Requirements

- ffmpeg (video processing)
- yt-dlp (platform integration)
