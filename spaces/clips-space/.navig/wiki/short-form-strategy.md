# Short-Form Strategy

> Content strategy for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

## Core Principles

1. **Hook first** — The first 3 seconds determine everything
2. **Vertical native** — Always 9:16, never cropped landscape
3. **Platform-native** — Adapt tone, pacing, and style per platform
4. **Volume + quality** — Consistent posting beats occasional perfection
5. **Trend-aware** — Ride trends early, add your unique angle

## Content Pillars

Define 3-5 content pillars that your clips rotate through:

| Pillar | Description | Example Type |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| Educational | Teach something in 30s | Tips, tutorials, how-tos |
| Entertaining | Make them laugh/react | Skits, reactions, challenges |
| Inspirational | Motivate or inspire | Transformations, stories |
| Behind-the-scenes | Show the process | Day-in-life, making-of |
| Trending | Ride viral formats | Sounds, challenges, duets |

## Posting Cadence

| Level | Frequency | Platforms |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Starter | 3x/week | 1 platform |
| Growth | 1x/day | 2 platforms |
| Scale | 2-3x/day | 3 platforms |

## Content Lifecycle

1. **Ideation** → Trend scan + content calendar
2. **Scripting** → Hook + body + CTA (under 60s)
3. **Production** → Film vertical, natural lighting, clear audio
4. **Editing** → Tight cuts, captions, effects, sound sync
5. **Publishing** → Platform adaption, hashtags, optimal time
6. **Analytics** → Track within 24h, iterate

## Repurposing Strategy

One piece of long-form content → multiple short-form clips:

- YouTube video → 3-5 clips (key moments, quotes, tips)
- Podcast episode → 2-3 clips (best stories, insights)
- Blog post → 1-2 clips (visual walkthrough, key takeaway)
- Livestream → 3-5 clips (highlights, Q&A answers)

## Audience Growth Levers

1. **Hooks** — Better hooks = higher 3-second retention = algorithm boost
2. **Consistency** — Daily posting signals active creator
3. **Trends** — Trending sounds give distribution boost on TikTok
4. **Series** — Multi-part content drives follows for "next episode"
5. **Engagement** — Reply to comments, ask questions, create duets
