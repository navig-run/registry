# Hashtag Strategy

> Research, selection, and optimisation of hashtags for short-form platforms.

## Hashtag Purpose

Hashtags on short-form platforms serve:
1. **Discoverability** — Help new viewers find your content
2. **Categorisation** — Tell the algorithm what your content is about
3. **Trend association** — Connect to trending topics for distribution boost

## The Mix Strategy

Use a balanced mix of hashtag sizes:

| Type | Volume | % of Set | Example |
|------|--------|----------|---------|
| **Broad** | 1M+ posts | 30% | #productivity, #tips |
| **Medium** | 100K-1M | 40% | #productivityhacks, #worktips |
| **Niche** | 10K-100K | 30% | #solofounder, #devlife |

## Platform-Specific Rules

### TikTok
- Use 3-5 hashtags (quality over quantity)
- Include 1 trending hashtag when relevant
- Place in caption, not comments
- Don't use #fyp #foryou (debated, may not help)

### Instagram Reels
- Use 10-15 hashtags
- Mix in caption and first comment
- Avoid banned/spam hashtags
- Update sets monthly (avoid shadowban from repetition)

### YouTube Shorts
- Use 2-3 hashtags maximum
- Always include #Shorts
- Keep hashtags in title or description
- Focus on searchable keywords

## Research Process

1. **Seed keywords**: Start with your topic/niche
2. **Platform search**: Type seed into platform search bar
3. **Related hashtags**: Note suggestions and related tags
4. **Competitor audit**: Check top creators' hashtag sets
5. **Volume check**: Verify post count (broad/medium/niche)
6. **Build sets**: Create 5-10 rotating sets of hashtags

## Monitoring

- Track which hashtag sets correlate with higher reach
- Remove underperforming hashtags monthly
- Add emerging niche hashtags as they grow
- Never use the same exact set on consecutive posts
