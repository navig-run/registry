# Trend Guide

> How to identify, evaluate, and ride short-form video trends.

## Trend Lifecycle

```
Emerging → Rising → Peak → Plateau → Decline
  (ride)    (ride)  (last chance)  (too late)  (skip)
```

**Rule**: Ride trends in the Emerging or Rising phase. By Peak, competition is high.

## How to Spot Trends

### Platform Signals

| Signal | Where to Find | What It Means |
|--------|--------------|---------------|
| Sound usage spike | TikTok sound page | Rising trend |
| Format repetition | For You / Explore | Format going viral |
| Hashtag velocity | Search / Discover | Topic gaining traction |
| Creator adoption | Following feed | Early movers catching on |

### External Tools

- TikTok Creative Center — trending sounds and hashtags
- Instagram Reels trending audio — in-app search
- YouTube Shorts trending — trending tab
- Google Trends — macro topic trends

## Trend Evaluation Criteria

Score each trend 1-5:

| Criteria | Question |
|----------|----------|
| **Velocity** | How fast is it growing? |
| **Relevance** | Does it fit our niche? |
| **Lifespan** | How long will it last (days/weeks)? |
| **Competition** | How many creators are already on it? |
| **Adaptability** | Can we add our unique angle? |

**Score > 18**: Ride immediately
**Score 12-18**: Plan within 48 hours
**Score < 12**: Monitor or skip

## How to Ride a Trend

1. **Identify** the trend (sound, format, or challenge)
2. **Add your angle** — never just copy; bring your niche
3. **Script quickly** — trend-riding clips should be produced in < 4 hours
4. **Use the trending sound** — even if it's background, the algorithm rewards it
5. **Use relevant hashtags** — include trend-specific + niche hashtags
6. **Post ASAP** — timing matters more than polish for trends

## Trend Types

| Type | Example | Speed Required |
|------|---------|----------------|
| Sound trend | New viral song/audio | < 24 hours |
| Format trend | "POV" or "Get Ready With Me" | < 48 hours |
| Challenge | Dance or task challenge | < 48 hours |
| Topic trend | News or cultural moment | < 12 hours |
| Seasonal | Holidays, events | Plan 1 week ahead |

## When NOT to Ride

- Trend is already declining (plateau/decline)
- Can't authentically connect to your niche
- Requires controversial or risky content
- Would dilute your content pillars
- Copyright/licensing issues with the sound
