# GENESIS — Clips Space

> **Short-Form Video Factory** — Create, optimise, and distribute TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts at scale.

## Origin

Short-form video dominates social media. Clips Space was built for creators who need to produce high-volume, high-quality short-form content across multiple platforms. Whether you're repurposing long-form content, riding trends, or batch-producing originals — this space provides the AI agents, workflows, and skills to move at platform speed.

## Agents

| Agent | Role | Specialty |
|-------|------|-----------|
| **clip-director** | Creative Lead | Content strategy, quality control, batch planning |
| **hook-writer** | Hook Specialist | First 3 seconds, captions, CTAs |
| **clip-editor** | Video Editor | Trimming, effects, format adaptation |
| **trend-scout** | Trend Analyst | Trending sounds, formats, hashtags |
| **distributor** | Multi-Platform Publisher | Cross-posting, scheduling, analytics |

## Formations

- **clip-factory** — End-to-end clip production (concept → publish)
- **trend-ride** — Rapid trend response (capture trending format/sound)
- **repurpose-lab** — Convert long-form content into shorts
- **batch-schedule** — Batch plan and schedule a week of clips
- **performance-review** — Cross-platform analytics and optimisation
