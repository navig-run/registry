# GENESIS — Growth Space

## Why This Exists

Growth is the one investment that compounds forever — but only if you're intentional about it. Growth Space is the personal OS layer for learning, habits, and self-improvement: tracking what you're building, what you're practicing, and who you're becoming.

Without it: good intentions accumulate in scattered notes, goals get abandoned by February, and skill-building happens at random. With it: every learning effort and habit is tracked, reviewed, and iterated on.

## What It Does

- **Habit tracking** — daily practice log for any habit you're building or breaking
- **Learning log** — books read, courses completed, skills practiced
- **Goal lifecycle** — from intention to milestone to outcome
- **Reflection cadence** — weekly review and monthly retrospective
- **Skill map** — a living map of what you know and what you're developing

## Origin

Built for operators who take personal growth seriously and want the same rigor they apply to work applied to their own development.
