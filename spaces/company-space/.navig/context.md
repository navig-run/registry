# Company Space — Context

## Mission

Run the entire business from one structured space: day-to-day operations, active project
delivery, financial management, client relations, decision tracking, and compliance.

## Domain Areas

1. **Operations** — SOPs, incident runbooks, vendor contacts, recurring workflows
2. **Project Delivery** — active project specs, milestones, ADRs, delivery artifacts
3. **Finance** — invoicing, expenses, revenue tracking, tax preparation, P&L
4. **Client Relations** — lead pipeline, proposals, contracts, onboarding, retention
5. **Decision Log** — architecture and business decision records with financial impact
6. **Compliance** — tax deadlines, regulatory obligations, document retention

## Guiding Principles

- One source of truth per topic — no duplicated docs
- Inbox zero: all items in `inbox/` are actioned or delegated within 48 hours
- Decisions are logged with rationale, financial impact, and reversibility
- Projects have explicit done criteria AND budget before they start
- All financial documents use sequential numbering with no gaps
- Revenue and expenses tracked against tax thresholds monthly
- Client relationships managed through defined lifecycle stages
