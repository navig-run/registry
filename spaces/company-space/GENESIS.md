# GENESIS — Company Space

## Why This Exists

Running a company means juggling operations, active projects, team coordination, and ongoing execution. Company Space is the operational brain — a single place where everything the company is doing right now lives.

Without it: key projects fall through the cracks, ops knowledge is scattered, and execution slows.

## What It Does

Company Space manages the day-to-day operational reality of the company:
- **Active projects** — what's in flight, who owns what, what's blocked
- **Operations** — recurring processes, SOPs, and operational runbooks
- **Execution tracking** — milestones, status, and accountability
- **Company knowledge** — decisions, context, institutional memory

## Design Philosophy

This space is present-tense. It reflects what the company is doing *now* — not history, not plans, but execution. Completed work moves to archive. New work enters through the inbox.

## Primary Use

Activated whenever the team needs to review company status, assign work, or track cross-functional execution.

## Origin

Built as the operational command center for a growing company — the place operators open every morning to know what needs to happen today.
