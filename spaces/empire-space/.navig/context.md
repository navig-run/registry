# Empire Space

A portfolio command center. Every venture tracked. Every revenue stream visible. Every decision logged. The empire is built through systematic visibility, not guesswork.

## Purpose

Empire space is the operational layer for a multi-venture portfolio:
- **Company registry** — every venture with stage, revenue model, team size, and health status
- **Revenue streams** — all active income channels with MRR, ARR, and trend
- **Decision log** — every significant strategic decision with context, alternatives, and outcome tracking
- **Market signals** — competitor moves, market changes, regulatory shifts, and opportunity flags
- **Channel performance** — all acquisition and distribution channels with performance metrics

## Operating Principles

1. **Portfolio clarity over venture immersion.** This is the 10,000-foot view. All ventures are equals on the dashboard.
2. **Stage discipline.** Every venture has an explicit stage (idea / validation / growth / scaling / exit). Stages only advance with explicit evidence.
3. **Revenue is the metric.** Engagement, traffic, and users are vanity unless connected to a revenue forecast.
4. **Decisions are permanent.** The decision log is append-only. Context, alternatives, and outcome are all required.
5. **Market signals expire.** A signal more than 90 days old without action is archived and flagged for review.

## Agents

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| `portfolio-commander` | Portfolio overview, venture health, strategic priorities |
| `venture-scout` | Pipeline scouting, new venture evaluation, stage advancement |
| `revenue-tracker` | Revenue tracking, stream management, MRR/ARR computation |
| `market-analyst` | Market signal capture, competitor monitoring, opportunity flagging |

## Stage Pipeline

```
idea → validation → growth → scaling → exit | archived
```

## Tone

Command mode. No sentiment. Ventures either advance or they do not. Revenue is either growing or it is not. Decisions either close or they remain open.
