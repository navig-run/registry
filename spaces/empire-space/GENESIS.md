# GENESIS — Empire Space

## Why This Exists

When you run multiple ventures simultaneously, the cognitive load of tracking them all is brutal. Empire Space is the portfolio command center — a single place to hold the strategic overview of every company, revenue stream, market signal, and cross-venture decision.

Without it: each venture gets managed in isolation, signal intelligence is siloed, decisions are repeated, and the portfolio has no coherent strategy.

## What It Does

Empire Space operates at the portfolio level:
- **Venture tracking** — status, stage, revenue, and strategic health of each venture
- **Revenue visibility** — all revenue streams tracked, attributed, and trended
- **Decision logging** — portfolio-level decisions recorded with context and outcomes
- **Market intelligence** — signals, trends, and competitive intelligence aggregated
- **Channel performance** — cross-venture channel analysis and optimization

## Core Agents

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| `portfolio-commander` | Oversees the full portfolio, drives strategic decisions |
| `venture-scout` | Scouts new opportunities and tracks venture pipeline |
| `revenue-tracker` | Tracks and forecasts revenue across all streams |
| `market-analyst` | Monitors signals, trends, and competitive landscape |

## Primary Formations

- **`portfolio-ops`** — Daily portfolio management and operations
- **`venture-review`** — Regular review of each venture's status and trajectory
- **`market-watch`** — Ongoing market signal monitoring and intelligence

## Origin

Built for operators running diversified portfolios who need a birds-eye view without losing the operational detail. The empire needs a map.
