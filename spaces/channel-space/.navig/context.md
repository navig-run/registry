# Channel Space — Context

## Purpose

YouTube creator command center providing AI-powered agents for video production, SEO optimisation, growth analytics, thumbnail testing, and community management.

## Scope

- Video scripting, editing, and publishing workflows
- YouTube SEO (titles, descriptions, tags, metadata)
- Thumbnail design and A/B testing
- Channel analytics and growth tracking
- Content calendar and batch planning
- Community engagement and collaboration

## Active Agents

- **showrunner** — Creative Director, oversees channel strategy
- **scriptwriter** — Scripts, hooks, outlines, show notes
- **editor** — Post-production, cuts, effects, exports
- **growth-analyst** — SEO, analytics, retention, CTR
- **art-director** — Thumbnails, branding, graphics

## Active Formations

- **video-production** — End-to-end video pipeline
- **channel-growth** — SEO and analytics operations
- **content-planning** — Batch content calendar
- **thumbnail-lab** — Thumbnail creation and testing
- **community-ops** — Audience engagement
