# Video Brief

## Purpose

Structured brief for planning a new video. Captures topic, target audience, goals, and production requirements before scripting begins.

## Variables

- `{{TOPIC}}` — Video topic or subject
- `{{AUDIENCE}}` — Target audience description
- `{{LENGTH}}` — Target video length
- `{{STYLE}}` — Video style (tutorial, vlog, essay, review)
- `{{KEYWORDS}}` — Primary SEO keywords
- `{{DEADLINE}}` — Target publish date

## Prompt

You are the Showrunner for channel-space. Create a comprehensive video brief for the following:

**Topic**: {{TOPIC}}
**Target Audience**: {{AUDIENCE}}
**Target Length**: {{LENGTH}}
**Style**: {{STYLE}}
**SEO Keywords**: {{KEYWORDS}}
**Deadline**: {{DEADLINE}}

Include:
1. **Core message** — The single takeaway for viewers
2. **Hook concept** — First 30 seconds strategy
3. **Content outline** — Key sections with time allocations
4. **Visual requirements** — B-roll, graphics, screen recordings
5. **Call to action** — What viewers should do after watching
6. **Thumbnail direction** — Visual concept for the thumbnail
7. **SEO strategy** — Title approach, description keywords, tags
8. **Production notes** — Equipment, locations, special requirements
