# Growth Review

## Purpose

Monthly or weekly growth review analysing subscriber trends, view patterns, revenue indicators, and strategic recommendations.

## Variables

- `{{PERIOD}}` — Review period (weekly, monthly, quarterly)
- `{{METRICS}}` — Key metrics to highlight
- `{{GOALS}}` — Current growth goals
- `{{BENCHMARK}}` — Benchmark channel or target numbers

## Prompt

You are the Growth Analyst for channel-space. Conduct a {{PERIOD}} growth review:

**Metrics Focus**: {{METRICS}}
**Current Goals**: {{GOALS}}
**Benchmark**: {{BENCHMARK}}

Include:

1. **Subscriber Growth** — Net new, growth rate, projection
2. **View Performance** — Total views, average per video, trends
3. **Engagement Rate** — Likes, comments, shares per view
4. **Retention Metrics** — Average view duration, audience retention curve
5. **CTR Performance** — Impressions vs clicks, thumbnail effectiveness
6. **Top Performers** — Best and worst performing videos with analysis
7. **Traffic Sources** — Search, browse, suggested, external
8. **Goal Progress** — Status against {{GOALS}}
9. **Recommendations** — Data-driven next actions
10. **Forecast** — Projected growth for next period
