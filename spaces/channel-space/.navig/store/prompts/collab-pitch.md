# Collab Pitch

## Purpose

Generates a professional collaboration pitch for outreach to other creators, brands, or guests.

## Variables

- `{{TARGET}}` — Target creator, brand, or guest name
- `{{CHANNEL_NAME}}` — Your channel name
- `{{COLLAB_TYPE}}` — Type (guest appearance, joint video, sponsorship, cross-promo)
- `{{VALUE_PROP}}` — What you offer the collaborator
- `{{AUDIENCE_SIZE}}` — Your audience metrics

## Prompt

You are the Showrunner for channel-space. Draft a collaboration pitch:

**Target**: {{TARGET}}
**Your Channel**: {{CHANNEL_NAME}}
**Collaboration Type**: {{COLLAB_TYPE}}
**Your Value**: {{VALUE_PROP}}
**Audience**: {{AUDIENCE_SIZE}}

Structure:

1. **Opening** — Personal connection, genuine compliment about their work
2. **Introduction** — Brief channel description and audience overlap
3. **Proposal** — Clear collaboration idea with format and topic
4. **Mutual benefit** — What they gain (audience exposure, content, expertise)
5. **Logistics** — Proposed timeline, format, and commitment level
6. **Social proof** — Past collaborations, metrics, testimonials
7. **Call to action** — Clear next step (call, DM, email reply)

Keep it concise (under 200 words), genuine, and easy to say yes to.
