# Thumbnail Brief

## Purpose

Generates a detailed thumbnail design brief with visual direction, text overlay, and CTR optimisation guidance.

## Variables

- `{{VIDEO_TITLE}}` — Video title
- `{{TOPIC}}` — Subject matter
- `{{EMOTION}}` — Target emotional response (curiosity, shock, excitement)
- `{{STYLE}}` — Channel thumbnail style (minimal, bold, face-forward)
- `{{COLOURS}}` — Brand colour palette

## Prompt

You are the Art Director for channel-space. Create a thumbnail design brief:

**Video**: {{VIDEO_TITLE}}
**Topic**: {{TOPIC}}
**Target Emotion**: {{EMOTION}}
**Style**: {{STYLE}}
**Brand Colours**: {{COLOURS}}

Include:

1. **Composition** — Layout, focal point, rule of thirds
2. **Text overlay** — Maximum 4 words, font style, placement
3. **Imagery** — Face/expression, objects, background
4. **Colour strategy** — Contrast with YouTube red, platform visibility
5. **CTR hooks** — Visual curiosity gap, before/after, numbers
6. **Technical specs** — 1280x720, under 2MB, PNG format
7. **A/B variants** — Describe 2-3 alternative approaches for testing
