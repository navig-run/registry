## Content Types

| Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Tutorial | Weekly | Teach a skill, solve a problem |
| Vlog | Bi-weekly | Build personal connection |
| Review | Monthly | Leverage product interest |
| List/Roundup | Monthly | High search volume, evergreen |
| Shorts | 3x/week | Discoverability, funnel to long-form |

## Audience Targeting

- **Primary audience**: Age, interests, skill level
- **Secondary audience**: Adjacent demographics
- **Viewer journey**: Discovery → Subscribe → Engage → Advocate

## Publishing Rhythm

- **Ideal schedule**: Consistency beats frequency
- **Minimum viable**: 1 long-form per week + 2-3 shorts
- **Peak times**: Test and iterate — check analytics for your audience
- **Batch production**: Film multiple videos in one session

## Content Lifecycle

1. **Idea** → Content calendar
2. **Research** → Keyword + competitor analysis
3. **Script** → Outline → Full script → Hook refinement
4. **Film** → Raw footage + B-roll
5. **Edit** → Assembly → Review → Final cut
6. **Optimise** → Title, description, tags, thumbnail
7. **Publish** → Upload, schedule, community post
8. **Analyse** → 48h CTR check, 7d retention review, 30d SEO audit
