# YouTube SEO Guide

## Title Optimisation

- **Length**: 50-60 characters (visible on all devices)
- **Keywords first**: Front-load the primary keyword
- **Numbers work**: "7 Tips", "Top 10", "in 5 Minutes"
- **Emotional triggers**: "Amazing", "Surprising", "You Won't Believe"
- **Avoid**: ALL CAPS (except 1-2 words), clickbait that doesn't deliver

### Title Formulas

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| How to [Result] in [Timeframe] | How to Edit Videos in 10 Minutes |
| [Number] [Topic] Tips for [Audience] | 7 SEO Tips for New YouTubers |
| [Topic]: [Benefit] | YouTube SEO: Double Your Views |
| Why [Surprising Claim] | Why Most YouTubers Fail at SEO |
| [Topic] vs [Topic]: Which is Better? | Premiere Pro vs DaVinci Resolve |

## Description Best Practices

- **First 2 lines**: Most important — visible without "Show More"
- **Include primary keyword** in first 25 words
- **Timestamps**: Add chapters for videos over 10 minutes
- **Links**: Social media, related videos, resources
- **Hashtags**: 3-5 relevant hashtags (shown above title)
- **Length**: 200-500 words for best SEO performance

## Tags Strategy

- **Primary tag**: Exact target keyword
- **Secondary tags**: Variations and long-tail keywords
- **Broad tags**: Channel category and niche
- **Competitor tags**: Keywords competitors rank for
- **Limit**: YouTube allows up to 500 characters

## Metadata Checklist

- [ ] Title under 60 characters with primary keyword
- [ ] Description: first 2 lines compelling, keywords in first 25 words
- [ ] Timestamps added for videos over 10 minutes
- [ ] 15-30 relevant tags covering broad and specific keywords
- [ ] Custom thumbnail uploaded (1280x720, under 2MB)
- [ ] Cards and end screens configured
- [ ] Correct category selected
- [ ] Language and caption settings confirmed
