# Analytics Reference

## Key Metrics

| Metric | What It Means | Good Benchmark |
|--------|---------------|----------------|
| CTR (Click-Through Rate) | % of impressions that become views | 4-10% |
| AVD (Average View Duration) | Average time watched | >50% of video length |
| Retention Rate | How much of the video people watch | >40% average |
| Subscriber Conversion | Views that lead to subscribes | 1-3% |
| RPM (Revenue Per Mille) | Revenue per 1000 views | Varies by niche |
| Impressions | Times your thumbnail was shown | Growing month-over-month |

## Traffic Sources

| Source | Description | Optimisation |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Browse | YouTube homepage recommendations | Strong CTR + retention |
| Search | YouTube and Google search results | SEO (title, description, tags) |
| Suggested | Sidebar and end screen suggestions | Related metadata + retention |
| External | From other websites, social media | Social sharing, embeds |
| Notifications | Subscriber bell notifications | Upload consistency |

## Retention Curve Patterns

| Pattern | What It Means | Action |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| Flat line at 50%+ | Strong engagement | Keep this format |
| Sharp drop at 0:30 | Hook isn't working | Rewrite opening hook |
| Gradual decline | Normal falloff | Add pattern interrupts |
| Spike then drop | Clickbait mismatch | Align title with content |
| Drop at mid-point | Content sags | Tighten the middle section |
| Rise at end | Strong close/CTA | Use more end cards |

## Review Schedule

| When | What | Action |
|------|------|--------|
| 2 hours post-publish | Initial CTR | Swap thumbnail if below 3% |
| 48 hours | CTR stabilisation | Evaluate title/thumbnail combo |
| 7 days | Retention + suggested traffic | Assess content quality |
| 30 days | Full performance | SEO audit, long-term potential |
| 90 days | Evergreen check | Update description/tags if trending |

## Channel Health Indicators

- **Healthy**: CTR >5%, AVD >50%, growing impressions
- **Warning**: CTR dropping, flat subscriber growth, declining AVD
- **Critical**: CTR <2%, AVD <30%, shrinking impressions
