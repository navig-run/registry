# Channel Space — Genesis

> YouTube Creator Command Center

## Origin

Born from the need to bring structure, consistency, and AI-powered efficiency to YouTube content creation. Channel Space treats your YouTube channel like a professional media operation — from scripting to analytics.

## What It Does

- **Video Production Pipeline** — Script → film → edit → publish with agent oversight
- **SEO Optimisation** — Titles, descriptions, tags, and metadata for discoverability
- **Thumbnail Testing** — A/B score thumbnails before publishing
- **Growth Analytics** — Track subscribers, retention, CTR, and revenue trends
- **Content Planning** — Batch-plan weeks of content with a calendar-driven approach
- **Community Management** — Monitor comments, engage audiences, manage collaborations

## Core Agents

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| **showrunner** | Creative Director — oversees the entire channel strategy |
| **scriptwriter** | Content Writer — scripts, hooks, outlines, show notes |
| **editor** | Post-Production Lead — cuts, effects, pacing, exports |
| **growth-analyst** | Data Analyst — SEO, analytics, retention, CTR |
| **art-director** | Visual Designer — thumbnails, branding, graphics |

## Formations

- **video-production** — End-to-end single video workflow
- **channel-growth** — SEO audit, analytics, competitive analysis
- **content-planning** — Batch content calendar and scheduling
- **thumbnail-lab** — Thumbnail creation and A/B testing
- **community-ops** — Audience engagement and collaboration
