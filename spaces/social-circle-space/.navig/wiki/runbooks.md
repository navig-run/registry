# Social Circle Space — Runbooks

## Weekly Relationship Check

**Frequency:** Weekly (5 minutes)

1. Open `#warmth` inbox
2. Review who's flagged
3. For each: decide to reach out or snooze
4. Make the outreach (outside this space)
5. Log the interaction after

---

## Import from LinkedIn

1. Export LinkedIn connections as CSV
2. Drop in `#contacts` inbox
3. contact-organizer processes: deduplicates, suggests circles
4. Review suggestions in batches (10-20 at a time)
5. Confirm circle assignments

---

## Quarterly Circle Review

**Frequency:** Every 3 months

1. Review each circle membership
2. Flag anyone who has moved circles (ex-colleague now friend, etc.)
3. Archive contacts with no interactions in >2 years
4. Review warmth thresholds — adjust if lifestyle has changed
