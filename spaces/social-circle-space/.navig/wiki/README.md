## Circle Design Principles

1. **Own your circles** — you define what each circle means to you
2. **A contact can be in multiple circles** — work friend is both work and friend
3. **Circles are not hierarchical** — inner circle ≠ "best" people, just closest
4. **Review quarterly** — people change circles naturally

## Relationship Warmth Model

| Circle | Cold after | Check-in cadence |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| Inner circle | 30 days | Monthly minimum |
| Work | 60 days | Every 2 months |
| Friends | 90 days | Quarterly |
| Community | 180 days | Twice a year |
| Online | 365 days | Annually |
