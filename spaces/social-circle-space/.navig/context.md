# Social Circle Space — Context

Personal relationship graph for managing your entire human network.

## Core Entities
- **Contact**: A person you know
- **Circle**: A group of contacts sharing a life context
- **Interaction**: A recorded touchpoint with a contact
- **Connection**: A relationship between two contacts in your network
- **Tag**: A label for organizing contacts

## Default Circles
- Inner Circle (closest relationships)
- Work (professional contacts)
- Family
- Friends
- Community (local / interest groups)
- Online (digital-only relationships)
