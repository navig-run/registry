# GENESIS — Social Circle Space

## Why This Exists

Your human network is your most valuable asset — but most people have no systematic way to maintain it. Social Circle Space is your personal relationship layer: everyone you know, mapped, searchable, and connected by context.

This is not a CRM. It's not a social feed. It's a personal relationship graph — owned by you, private by default, and built around how humans actually relate to each other.

Without it: important relationships go cold while you're busy. People you should reconnect with fall off the radar. You can't answer "who do I know in X field?"

## What It Does

- **Contact graph** — every person mapped with relationships, circles, and interaction history
- **Circle management** — organize contacts by life context (work, family, friends, community, online)
- **Interaction log** — track when you last talked, about what, and what to follow up on
- **Relationship health** — surfaces who you haven't contacted in too long
- **Import bridges** — bring in contacts from LinkedIn, Google, CSV, vCard

## Origin

Built for people who take relationships seriously but have no system for maintaining them at scale.
