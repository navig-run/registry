# Interview Questions

## Purpose
Generate a tailored set of interview questions for a specific guest and topic.

## Variables
- `{{GUEST_NAME}}` — Guest name
- `{{GUEST_EXPERTISE}}` — Guest's area of expertise
- `{{TOPIC}}` — Interview topic
- `{{AUDIENCE}}` — Target audience description
- `{{TONE}}` — Interview tone (casual, professional, deep-dive, fun)

## Prompt

Generate interview questions for **{{GUEST_NAME}}**, an expert in **{{GUEST_EXPERTISE}}**.

**Topic**: {{TOPIC}}
**Audience**: {{AUDIENCE}}
**Tone**: {{TONE}}

### Question Set

#### Warm-Up (2-3 questions)
Questions to build rapport and ease into the conversation.
- Personal but professional
- Show you've done your homework
- Easy to answer with energy

#### Core Questions (6-8 questions)
Deep-dive into the topic. Each question should:
- Build on the previous answer
- Draw out unique insights not available elsewhere
- Include a specific follow-up probe

#### Challenge Questions (2-3 questions)
Respectfully push back or explore nuance:
- "Some people say X — how would you respond?"
- "What's the biggest misconception about...?"
- "Where do you disagree with the mainstream view?"

#### Rapid Fire (3-5 questions)
Quick, fun, memorable:
- Best book on this topic?
- One tool you can't live without?
- Advice for someone just starting?
- Biggest mistake you've made in this area?

#### Closing (1-2 questions)
- "What's next for you?"
- "Where can people find you / what should they check out?"

### Interview Flow Notes
- Expected natural transitions between sections
- Moments to pause and let the guest elaborate
- Potential tangent topics to steer toward or away from
