# Guest Prep

## Purpose
Prepare a comprehensive guest brief with background research and interview strategy.

## Variables
- `{{GUEST_NAME}}` — Guest full name
- `{{GUEST_ROLE}}` — Guest's current role/title
- `{{TOPIC}}` — Interview topic
- `{{EPISODE_NUMBER}}` — Episode number
- `{{RESEARCH_NOTES}}` — Raw research notes about the guest

## Prompt

Prepare a complete guest brief for **{{GUEST_NAME}}** ({{GUEST_ROLE}}) appearing on **Episode {{EPISODE_NUMBER}}**.

**Interview Topic**: {{TOPIC}}

### 1. Guest Bio
- Professional background (2-3 paragraphs)
- Notable achievements
- Current projects and focus areas

### 2. Previous Appearances
- List of previous podcast/media appearances (if found)
- Key quotes or positions they've taken publicly
- Topics they've covered before (avoid repetition)

### 3. Interview Strategy
- **Angle**: What unique perspective can we draw out?
- **Arc**: How should the conversation flow?
- **Moment**: What's the one "highlight clip" moment we're aiming for?

### 4. Interview Questions
Provide 15 questions in interview order:

| # | Question | Purpose | Follow-up |
|---|----------|---------|-----------|

Categories:
- **Warm-up** (1-3): Easy, personal, rapport-building
- **Core** (4-10): Deep-dive into topic expertise
- **Challenge** (11-12): Push back or explore controversy
- **Personal** (13-14): Behind-the-scenes, personal growth
- **Close** (15): Forward-looking, memorable final thought

### 5. Topics to Avoid
- Sensitive subjects or areas they've declined before
- Competitor mentions that could be uncomfortable
- Outdated or incorrect information to verify

### 6. Logistics
- Recording date and time
- Platform (Zoom, Riverside, in-person)
- Technical requirements for guest
- Pre-recording checklist
