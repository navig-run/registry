# Season Plan

## Purpose
Plan a complete podcast season with episode arc, themes, and production schedule.

## Variables
- `{{SHOW_NAME}}` — Podcast name
- `{{SEASON_NUMBER}}` — Season number
- `{{EPISODE_COUNT}}` — Number of episodes in the season
- `{{THEME}}` — Season theme or overarching topic
- `{{START_DATE}}` — Season premiere date
- `{{RELEASE_CADENCE}}` — Release frequency (weekly, biweekly)

## Prompt

Plan **Season {{SEASON_NUMBER}}** of **{{SHOW_NAME}}**.

**Theme**: {{THEME}}
**Episodes**: {{EPISODE_COUNT}}
**Start Date**: {{START_DATE}}
**Cadence**: {{RELEASE_CADENCE}}

### 1. Season Overview
- Season thesis: one sentence capturing the season arc
- What will the listener gain by the end?
- How does this season build on previous ones?

### 2. Episode Outline

### 3. Season Arc
- **Opening** (Ep 1-2): Set the stage, introduce the theme
- **Build** (Ep 3-6): Deep-dive into subtopics
- **Climax** (Ep 7-8): Most compelling guest or revelation
- **Resolution** (Ep 9-10): Synthesis, looking forward
