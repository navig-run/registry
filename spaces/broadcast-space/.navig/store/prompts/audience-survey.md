# Audience Survey

## Purpose
Create listener survey questions to gather feedback and guide content strategy.

## Variables
- `{{SHOW_NAME}}` — Podcast name
- `{{SURVEY_GOAL}}` — What you want to learn (content preferences, format feedback, etc.)
- `{{EPISODE_COUNT}}` — Number of episodes published
- `{{AUDIENCE_SIZE}}` — Approximate listener count

## Prompt

Create a listener survey for **{{SHOW_NAME}}** ({{EPISODE_COUNT}} episodes, ~{{AUDIENCE_SIZE}} listeners).

**Survey Goal**: {{SURVEY_GOAL}}

### Survey Questions

#### Listening Habits (3-4 questions)
- How did you discover the show?
- How often do you listen? (every episode / most / occasionally / just started)
- Where do you listen? (Apple / Spotify / YouTube / Other)
- Do you listen at normal speed or sped up?

#### Content Preferences (4-5 questions)
- What topics do you want more of? (multiple choice)
- Preferred episode format? (solo / interview / panel / mix)
- Preferred episode length? (15min / 30min / 45min / 60min+)
- Rate recent episodes 1-5
- What's the #1 thing you'd change?

#### Engagement (2-3 questions)
- Have you recommended the show to someone?
- Do you follow on social media?
- Would you join a community (Discord, newsletter)?

#### Open-Ended (2 questions)
- What's one episode idea you'd love to hear?
- Any other feedback?

### Survey Distribution Plan
- Best platforms and tools to collect responses
- Incentive suggestions (bonus episode, shoutout, etc.)
- Target response rate and follow-up timeline
