# Broadcast Space — Store Preview

## 🎙️ Broadcast Space

**Podcast & Live Streaming Command Center**

Professional space for podcast production, live streaming, guest management, and multi-platform audio distribution.

### Key Features

- **Episode production pipeline** — Record, edit, master, publish in one workflow
- **Guest management** — Research, outreach, scheduling, prep, and follow-up
- **Live streaming support** — Pre-show checklists, engagement monitoring, clip extraction
- **RSS & distribution** — Automated feed generation, multi-platform publishing
- **AI transcription** — Auto-generate transcripts and show notes
- **Social clip extraction** — Pull highlights for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts

### Who Is This For?

- Podcast hosts and producers
- Live streamers (Twitch, YouTube Live, Twitter Spaces)
- Audio content creators
- Interview-format show runners
- Teams managing multiple shows
