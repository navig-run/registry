# 🎙️ Broadcast Space — Genesis

## Origin

Created as a NAVIG Community Flagship space for podcast producers, live streamers, and audio content creators who need a structured production environment.

## Philosophy

Broadcasting is about consistent, high-quality audio content delivered on a predictable schedule. This space treats every episode as a production — from guest research to final distribution — with agents that handle each phase of the workflow.

## Why This Exists

1. **Episode production is complex** — recording, editing, mixing, show notes, transcripts, distribution
2. **Guest management is a pipeline** — research, outreach, scheduling, prep, interview, follow-up
3. **Live streaming adds real-time pressure** — setup checklists, engagement monitoring, clip extraction
4. **Multi-platform distribution** — RSS, Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, social clips
5. **Growth requires analytics** — listener trends, episode performance, audience engagement

## Design Decisions

- **Host leads** — The show host agent is the creative lead and decision maker
- **Audio-first** — Video is optional; audio quality is never compromised
- **Guest pipeline** — Built as a first-class workflow, not an afterthought
- **RSS native** — Feed generation and validation integrated into every publish
- **Clip extraction** — Every episode produces social clips automatically

## What This Is Not

- Not a music production space (see studio-space)
- Not a video-first editing suite (see clips-space)
- Not a streaming overlay tool — it manages the content, not the OBS setup
