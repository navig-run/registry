# Learn Space — Context

## Domain

Personal learning — courses, books, tutorials, skill development.
Every learning item belongs to a skill area and feeds a knowledge base.

## Content Model

- **Learning item**: a course, book, tutorial, or series being consumed
- **Insight**: an extracted, actionable piece of knowledge
- **Skill**: a capability being developed (groups multiple learning items)
- **Review**: a scheduled retrieval event (spaced repetition)

## What Lives Here

- Course and book tracking (progress, notes, completion)
- Insight library — organized by skill
- Skill profiles — current level, target level, evidence
- Spaced repetition queue — what to review and when
- Weekly review logs

## Principles

1. An insight not reviewed is an insight not retained.
2. Skills are demonstrated, not just studied.
3. Finish rate matters — track and surface abandoned items.
4. Progress is measured by skill level, not content consumed.

## Boundaries

- Academic coursework with formal deadlines → project-space for deadline tracking
- Entertainment reading → not tracked unless insights are captured
- Conference notes → keep only if processed into insights
