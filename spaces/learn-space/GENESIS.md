# GENESIS — Learn Space

## Why This Space Exists

Most learning is wasted. Courses started and never finished. Books read and
promptly forgotten. Skills acquired but never practiced into real capability.
The Learn Space exists to fix that — turning consuming into retaining, and
retaining into doing.

## Design Principles

1. **Insight is the unit, not content.** A book isn't done when you finish reading it.
   It's done when you've extracted and tested the insights that matter to you.

2. **Active recall over passive review.** Every captured insight becomes a quiz
   question. Knowledge is confirmed through retrieval, not re-reading.

3. **Track what you're building, not just what you're consuming.** Every learning
   item belongs to a skill or knowledge area. The system shows skill progress,
   not just a list of completed courses.

4. **Spaced repetition is non-negotiable.** Review schedules are assigned
   automatically. Items not reviewed on schedule are surfaced, not silently dropped.

5. **Finish rate matters.** Abandoned courses are tracked. The system gently surfaces
   items that have gone stale, and helps decide: continue, abandon, or pause.

## Scope

This space covers: online courses, books (non-fiction), technical tutorials,
certifications, language learning, professional skills, personal development.

It does NOT cover: formal academic coursework with deadlines (→ use project-space
for deadline tracking), entertainment reading, podcasts unless deliberate.

## Core Cadences

- **Daily:** Capture insights during learning sessions
- **Weekly:** Review queue — surface spaced repetition items
- **Quarterly:** Skill assessment (`skill-assessment` workflow) — what's improving, what's stagnating
