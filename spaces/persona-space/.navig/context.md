# Persona Space — Context

Agent persona design, versioning, and sharing. Your persona defines how your agent thinks, speaks, and decides.

## Persona Components
- **Voice**: tone, formality, verbosity
- **Reasoning style**: analytical vs intuitive, risk tolerance
- **Decision heuristics**: what tradeoffs you always prefer
- **Domain expertise**: what you know deeply
- **Blind spots**: what to compensate for
