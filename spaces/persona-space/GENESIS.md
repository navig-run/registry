# GENESIS — Persona Space

## Why This Exists

Your agent is an extension of you. Persona Space is where you design exactly how it thinks, speaks, and makes decisions — and publish personas to the community so others can fork them.

Without it: agents use default system prompts with no personality or consistent judgment. With it: your agent sounds like you, reasons like you, and can be versioned and shared.

## What It Does

- **Persona design** — define voice, tone, reasoning style, and decision heuristics
- **Identity versioning** — save and diff persona versions as your style evolves
- **Persona gallery** — browse community-contributed personas
- **Fork + adapt** — take someone else's persona as a starting point
- **Vault storage** — private personas stored securely in the vault

## Origin

Built for operators who want their agent to be a genuine extension of their thinking, not a generic assistant.
