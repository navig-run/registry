# Social Media Space — Context

## Purpose

The operating nerve centre for all social media activity. Whether you manage one brand with one account or a portfolio of ten brands across eight platforms, this space is the single surface where content is planned, created, reviewed, scheduled, published, and learned from.

No platform tabs. No disconnected spreadsheets. No wondering what was posted when and why it did or didn't work.

## Domain Areas

1. **Brand Management** — each brand is a first-class object with its own voice, accounts, content pillars, and performance baseline
2. **Content Strategy** — editorial calendar, content pillars, posting frequency, platform-specific strategy per brand
3. **Content Production** — drafting, rewriting, platform adaptation, voice consistency enforcement
4. **Scheduling & Queue** — platform-aware scheduling with time-slot optimisation and conflict detection
5. **Analytics & Intelligence** — engagement tracking, growth curves, viral coefficient, cross-brand benchmarking
6. **Viral Intelligence** — pre-publish hook scoring, pattern recognition from best-performing posts, viral signal detection

## Core Objects

- **Brand** — the unit of organisation; everything belongs to a brand
- **Post** — the unit of content; always has brand, platform, status, pillar, hook_score
- **Account** — a platform identity belonging to a brand
- **Pillar** — a content theme category within a brand (education, entertainment, authority, conversion, community)
- **Campaign** — a coordinated multi-post moment across one or more platforms

## Supported Platforms

X/Twitter · Instagram · LinkedIn · TikTok · YouTube · Threads · Bluesky · Mastodon

## Guiding Principles

- **Brand integrity before speed** — a bad post that goes out fast destroys brand equity faster than a slow posting schedule
- **Consistency compounds** — regular posting in a consistent voice outperforms viral spikes followed by silence
- **Learn from every post** — analytics are not vanity metrics; they are feedback that shapes next week's strategy
- **Viral is a system, not luck** — hooks can be analysed, scored, and improved; this space does that automatically
- **Local-first intelligence** — all brand data, voice specifications, and analytics stay on your machine; publish explicitly, not automatically
