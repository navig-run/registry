# Platform Guide

Best practices, character limits, content formats, and posting strategy
for every platform supported in this space.

---

| Platform | Char Limit | Best Content | Best Timing | Hashtags |
|----------|-----------|--------------|-------------|----------|

## X / Twitter

**Content that works:**
- Single-sentence insight with implied consequence
- Numbered lists (1/ threads for complex ideas)
- Contrarian takes backed by a specific data point
- "Hot take + proof" format: bold claim → 1 supporting fact

**Hook rules:**
- First 12 words are the entire pitch — after that you've lost casual scrollers
- Numbers, percentages, and years outperform vague claims
- "Most people don't know that..." performs poorly — be specific about WHO

**Thread format:**
- Tweet 1: the hook (the best single thought you have)
- Tweets 2-N: supporting evidence, story, or framework
- Final tweet: CTA or summary — never leave the thread open-ended

**Hashtag strategy:**
- 1-2 hashtags max
- Only use if the hashtag has traffic (#SaaS, #ProductDesign, #BuildInPublic)
- Never use hashtags as sentences: `#excited #newpost #mustread`

## LinkedIn

**Content that works:**
- Personal story with a professional lesson
- Industry insight with counterintuitive conclusion
- "Here's what we learned after doing X" (candid, not promotional)
- Data-driven argument with clear practical takeaway

**Hook rules:**
- First line must stand alone before the "...see more" fold
- 210 characters max visible preview — use them wisely
- Opening with a question works here (unique to LinkedIn algorithm)
- Self-aware openers: "I was wrong about..." outperform aspirational openers

**Format:**
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max per paragraph)
- Line breaks between every idea
- Avoid bullet-heavy posts — paragraph form performs better
- End with a direct question to drive comments

**Hashtag strategy:**
- 3-5 hashtags at the bottom
- Mix: 1 niche (#B2BMarketing), 1 broad (#Leadership), 1 topic-specific

---

## Instagram

**Content that works:**
- High-quality visual is non-negotiable — caption supports the image
- Carousels (multi-slide) outperform single images for educational content
- Story arcs: "Before → During → After"
- Behind-the-scenes content drives saves

**Hook rules:**
- First 125 characters visible before "more" — front-load the emotional trigger
- Visual emoji at the start helps (if brand voice allows)
- Reels: hook must work as on-screen text AND as spoken audio

**Format:**
- Caption can be long (up to 2200 chars) — use it for storytelling
- Line breaks between paragraphs (add . on blank lines for spacing)
- Hashtags at the very end, separated by line breaks

**Hashtag strategy:**
- 5-10 hashtags — a mix of sizes
- 2 large (>1M posts): maximum reach
- 3 medium (100K–1M): best engagement-to-competition ratio
- 2-3 niche (<100K): highest conversion rate

## TikTok

**Content that works:**
- Conversational, direct-to-camera energy (even in text posts)
- "Get ready with me to learn..." style framing
- Hook in first 2 seconds (video) or first line (text)
- Trending audio alignment when on-brand

**Hook rules:**
- Hook must work as spoken text — test it aloud
- Curiosity gap is the dominant hook pattern here
- "POV: You finally figured out..." outperforms most other formats
- Avoid corporate language more aggressively than any other platform

**Format:**
- Short, punchy sentences
- Conversational punctuation (ellipses, em dashes for rhythm)
- Never write like a press release

---

## Threads

**Content that works:**
- Hot takes, reactive commentary, casual insights
- Shorter is usually better — under 150 characters performs well
- Community-first: acknowledge the conversation you're joining

**Hook rules:**
- DM energy — write like you're texting someone interesting
- No CTAs in first post — earn engagement with quality, then ask

**Hashtag strategy:** 0-2, only if clearly relevant

---

## Bluesky

**Content that works:**
- Direct, low-noise, respect for reader intelligence
- Technical community appreciates depth over polish
- Original ideas > repurposed content

**Hook rules:**
- 300 chars forces clarity — no filler
- Peer register: never "we're excited to announce"
- Avoid marketing language entirely

## Cross-Platform Repurposing

This space supports repurposing one post across platforms. Key rules:

| From → To | What to change |
|-----------|---------------|
| LinkedIn → Twitter | Strip 80% of body, keep only the hook + 1 data point |
| Twitter → LinkedIn | Expand the insight into a story with context |
| Long-form → Instagram | Extract the single strongest visual claim |
| Any → TikTok | Rewrite in spoken-word, conversational register |
| Any → Bluesky | Compress to 280 chars and remove all corporate language |

Use `@post-writer repurpose --from linkedin --to twitter --draft {file}` to trigger this.
