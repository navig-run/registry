## What This Space Does

- **Multi-brand, multi-account** — one per brand, many brands, any platform combination
- **Content pipeline** — from raw idea to published post, fully tracked
- **Brand voice enforcement** — every draft checked against brand voice before it ships
- **Scheduling queue** — platform-aware scheduling with optimal time slots
- **Analytics** — engagement tracking, growth curves, viral coefficient per brand/platform
- **Viral scoring** — pre-publish hook analysis so your best content gets pushed first
- **Cross-platform** — X/Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon
