# Human Space — Context

## Purpose

Self, managed like a system. Everything that makes you operate well — health,
habits, daily rhythm, personal growth, and administrative life — in one structured place.

## Domain Areas

1. **Daily Planning** — morning review, priority setting, end-of-day reflection, journaling
2. **Health & Wellness** — fitness tracking, sleep, nutrition, mood, mental health, body metrics
3. **Habits** — habit design (Atomic Habits framework), streak tracking, environment design
4. **Learning & Growth** — skill building, book notes, courses, reflection, retrospectives
5. **Journal** — daily thoughts, gratitude, mood notes, freeform reflection
6. **Bureau** — personal documents, IDs, subscriptions, appointments, renewals, insurance

## Guiding Principles

1. Systems over willpower — make the good path easy, not the hard path hard
2. Measure what matters — only track metrics that drive decisions
3. Weekly retrospective — review weekly, adjust monthly, reassess quarterly
4. Energy management first — protect rest and recovery as a first-class concern
5. No shame — miss a day? Restart. No guilt spiral, no judgment
6. Small wins compound — celebrate streaks, acknowledge progress
7. Bureau discipline — renewals and admin tasks don't manage themselves; schedule them
