# GENESIS — Human Space

## Why This Exists

You are the most important system you operate. Human Space is the life management layer — the space dedicated to your health, habits, personal growth, and self-knowledge.

Without it: personal goals compete with work noise and lose. Health data is scattered. Growth intentions stay intentions. The operator neglects the human.

## What It Does

Human Space manages the whole person:
- **Health** — physical health tracking, medical records, fitness, sleep, nutrition
- **Habits** — habit tracking, streaks, behavioral patterns, accountability
- **Personal growth** — learning goals, self-improvement plans, reflection journals
- **Self-knowledge** — energy patterns, emotional state, what makes you perform well
- **Bureau** — personal documents, IDs, subscriptions, appointments, administrative life

## Design Philosophy

This space is introspective and private. It exists to help you run yourself well — not to optimize productivity, but to sustain energy, clarity, and intention over the long term.

## Local Data Areas

Human Space keeps its working data in three folders under `spaces/`:
- **`spaces/health`** — Body metrics, fitness logs (medical scans go to `navig cabinet`)
- **`spaces/growth`** — Habits, learning log, journal, retros, book notes
- **`spaces/bureau`** — Renewals, subscriptions, appointments (document scans go to `navig cabinet`)

For more depth, install the standalone `health-space`, `growth-space` or `bureau-space`.

## Origin

Built because the operator's human needs were being crowded out by every other operational space. This is the counterweight — the space that asks "how are *you* doing?"
