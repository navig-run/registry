# GENESIS — DevOps Space

## Why This Exists

Infrastructure never sleeps. DevOps Space is the operations command center — a dedicated environment for managing CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, site reliability, and incident response.

Without it: deployments are manual, incidents escalate slowly, infrastructure state is unknown, and on-call is chaos.

## What It Does

DevOps Space owns the full infrastructure and delivery lifecycle:
- **CI/CD pipelines** — build, test, and deploy automation
- **Infrastructure-as-code** — Terraform, Ansible, and configuration management
- **SRE operations** — monitoring, alerting, SLO tracking
- **Incident response** — detection, triage, communication, and post-mortems
- **Security hardening** — patch management, vulnerability scanning, access control

## Design Philosophy

Automation-first. Every runbook should eventually become a workflow. Every incident should generate a retro. Every infra change should be reviewed before apply.

## Primary Formations

- **`ci-pipeline`** — Build and deployment pipeline management
- **`infra-deploy`** — Infrastructure provisioning and day-to-day IaC operations
- **`incident-response`** — Real-time incident triage and resolution
- **`sre-ops`** — Reliability reviews, SLOs and error budgets

## Origin

Built for DevOps and SRE engineers who need a dedicated operational environment that keeps infrastructure context always available.
