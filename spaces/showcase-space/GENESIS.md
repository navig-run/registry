# GENESIS — Showcase Space

## Why This Exists

The best NAVIG setups are built by the community. Showcase Space is the gallery — browse agents, packs, and formations made by other operators, demo them before installing, and install in 10 seconds.

Without it: every operator builds in isolation, never discovering what others have already solved. With it: the community's best work is one click from your instance.

## What It Does

- **Community gallery** — browse agents, packs, formations, and workflows from contributors
- **Preview before install** — see what something does before committing
- **One-click install** — install any community asset directly into your NAVIG instance
- **Ratings + reviews** — see what the community trusts
- **Contribute** — publish your own creations to the gallery

## Origin

Built to make NAVIG's ecosystem self-reinforcing — every great setup makes the whole community better.
