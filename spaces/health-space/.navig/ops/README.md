# Health Space — Ops

## Daily Log (3 minutes)
Post to `#daily`: sleep hours, sleep quality (1-10), energy (1-10), mood (1-10), water intake, notable events

## Log a Workout
Post to `#activity`: type, duration, intensity, how you felt after

## Log a Mental Health Day
Post to `#mental`: mood score, stress level, contributing factors

## Monthly Trend Review
1. Pull 30-day data for sleep, energy, mood
2. Identify: best days, worst days, patterns
3. Correlate: what activities/events correlate with high/low scores?
4. Adjust behavior or environment based on findings
