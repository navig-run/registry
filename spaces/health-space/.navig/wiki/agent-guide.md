# Health Space — Agent Guide

> **Planned roles — not shipped yet.** No agent definitions exist in `.navig/store/agents/`
> for this space; the roles below describe the intended design.

## health-monitor (Lead)
Synthesizes daily logs, detects patterns, and flags unusual signals worth attention.

## fitness-tracker
Manages activity logs, tracks workout consistency, and monitors fitness trends.

## mental-health-logger
Tracks mood, stress, and mental health signals. Surfaces mood patterns.

## sleep-analyst
Focuses specifically on sleep data — duration, quality, and correlation with energy/mood.
