# Health Space — Runbooks

## Daily Log

1. At day end, post to `#daily`:
   - Sleep last night: X hours, quality X/10
   - Today's energy: X/10
   - Today's mood: X/10
   - Water today: X liters
   - Activity: [if any]
   - Notes: [anything notable]
2. Takes 2 minutes. Do it consistently.

## Monthly Trend Review

1. Pull all daily logs for the month
2. Calculate averages: sleep, energy, mood
3. Compare to prior month
4. Identify outlier days: what made them high or low?
5. Extract one actionable insight
6. Apply next month
