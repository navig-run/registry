# Health Space — Context

Personal health tracking for physical and mental wellbeing. Tracks activity, nutrition, mood, and sleep.

## Purpose
Pattern awareness and early signal detection. Not a medical tool — a self-observation system.

## Primary Signals
- Sleep: duration and quality
- Energy: subjective 1-10 rating
- Mood: subjective 1-10 rating
- Activity: type, duration, intensity
- Hydration: liters per day
