# GENESIS — Health Space

## Why This Exists

Health is the foundation everything else runs on. Health Space is the personal tracking layer for physical and mental health — not as a medical tool, but as a self-awareness system that helps you notice patterns, maintain baselines, and catch signals before they become problems.

Without it: health data is scattered across three apps, forgotten by month three, and never connected to how you actually feel. With it: your health picture is integrated, historical, and yours.

## What It Does

- **Activity tracking** — workouts, movement, energy levels
- **Nutrition logging** — what you ate, hydration, supplements
- **Mental health signals** — mood, stress, sleep quality
- **Symptom journaling** — track and correlate physical signals
- **Trend visibility** — see patterns across days, weeks, months

## Origin

Built for operators who want to treat their health with the same intentionality they apply to work — not obsessively, but systematically.
