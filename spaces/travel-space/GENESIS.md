# GENESIS — Travel Space

## Why This Space Exists

Travel is one of the highest-ROI human activities — but only when the
logistics are handled without friction. The Travel Space exists to make
every trip from first idea through final debrief feel effortless, affordable,
and well-remembered.

## Design Principles

1. **Trip is the atomic unit.** Everything in this space belongs to a specific trip.
   Each trip has its own folder, its own budget, its own status.

2. **Pre-trip work is the real work.** A well-planned trip needs 80% of its thinking
   done before departure. This space front-loads the cognitive load.

3. **Budget visibility from day one.** When a trip is created it must have at least a
   rough budget target. Every booking updates the running total.

4. **Documents must never be scrambled for.** Passports, visas, insurance, hotel
   confirmations — all in one place, clearly labeled.

5. **Debrief captures what gets forgotten.** Notes written the day you return are
   10× more valuable than notes written a week later.

## Scope

This space covers: leisure travel, family trips, solo adventures, short weekend
breaks, conference travel with leisure component.

It does NOT cover: purely business travel (→ a work-focused space),
expense reimbursement tracking (→ finance-space).

## Core Cadences

- **New trip:** Define → estimate → book → pack → depart
- **Active trip:** Emergency contacts + documents always accessible
- **Post-trip:** Debrief within 48 hours, archive within one week
