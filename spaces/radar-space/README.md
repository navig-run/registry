# Cost Radar

> Resource costs, budget drift, cloud spend — tracked before they become surprises.

**Tier:** platform · **Status:** planned  
**Owner:** navig-run · **Created:** 2026-03-17

---

## Purpose

Cost overruns are almost always visible in hindsight. This space makes them
visible in advance: resource usage, spend by service, budget drift alerts, and
a monthly anomaly report that surfaces the number before it becomes a problem.

---

## What lives here

- Cost tracking logs and snapshots
- Budget definitions and thresholds
- Anomaly reports
- Service cost breakdowns
