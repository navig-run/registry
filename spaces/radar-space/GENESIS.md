# GENESIS — Radar Space

## Why This Exists

Cloud costs don't announce themselves — they accumulate silently until the monthly bill arrives. Radar Space is the cost intelligence layer: track every resource, detect anomalies early, and never be surprised by a bill again.

Without it: you reconcile cloud costs monthly, after the damage. With it: cost anomalies are flagged the day they appear.

## What It Does

- **Cost tracking** — all cloud and service costs in one consolidated view
- **Anomaly detection** — spike alerts when costs deviate from baseline
- **Budget enforcement** — set limits per service/team, alert at threshold
- **Trend analysis** — cost per unit of output over time
- **Monthly report** — cost summary card shareable with stakeholders

## Origin

Built for teams who discovered they'd spent 10x their expected cloud budget on a forgotten experiment. The kind of discovery that should happen in real-time, not at month-end.
