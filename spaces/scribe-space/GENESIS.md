# GENESIS — Scribe Space

## Why This Exists

Every word form deserves a dedicated space. Scribe Space is the writer's workshop — a structured environment for fiction, journalism, screenwriting, technical documentation, and content strategy, all under one roof.

Without it: drafts are scattered, editorial workflows are ad hoc, and the distance between idea and published work is too long.

## What It Does

Scribe Space covers the full writing craft spectrum:
- **Fiction** — novels, short stories, world-building, character development
- **Journalism** — article research, interviews, fact-checking, publication
- **Screenwriting** — screenplay formatting, scene development, production notes
- **Technical writing** — documentation, API references, user guides
- **Content strategy** — editorial calendars, brand voice, content audits

## Core Agents

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| `storyteller` | Fiction development and narrative craft |
| `journalist` | News writing, research, and fact-checking |
| `editor` | Editing, proofreading, and style guidance |
| `content-strategist` | Content planning and distribution |
| `screenwriter` | Screenplay formatting and story structure |
| `tech-writer` | Technical documentation and API docs |

## Primary Formations

- **`fiction-workshop`** — Active fiction writing and development
- **`journalism-workflow`** — Article research through publication
- **`content-pipeline`** — Content strategy and production
- **`production-draft`** — Final draft and delivery mode

## Origin

Built for writers who need their AI tools to understand the specific craft they're practising — not just "write something" but "write a third-act screenplay scene with mounting tension."
