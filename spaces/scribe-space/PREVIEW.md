# scribe-space — Preview

> Writers' space for fiction, journalism, screenwriting, technical docs, and content strategy.

## What's included

### 6 Agents
- **Storyteller** — long-form fiction, character development, chapter drafts
- **Journalist** — articles, investigations, interview briefs
- **Editor** — structural, line, and copy editing
- **Content Strategist** — editorial calendars, brand voice, content briefs
- **Screenwriter** — Fountain-format film and TV scripts
- **Tech Writer** — API reference, user guides, SOPs

### 4 Formations
`fiction-workshop` · `journalism-workflow` · `content-pipeline` · `production-draft`

### 5 Skills
`word-count` · `grammar-check` · `outline-draft` · `export-pdf` · `readability-score`

### 3 Workflows
`first-draft` · `editorial-review` · `publish-checklist`

### 6 Sub-workspaces
`book-writing/` · `journalism/` · `general-writing/` · `screenwriting/` · `technical-writing/` · `content-strategy/`

## Integrations
- LanguageTool (grammar/style API)
- Pandoc (PDF/DOCX/EPUB export)
- afterwriting (Fountain → PDF)
- textstat (local readability scoring)
