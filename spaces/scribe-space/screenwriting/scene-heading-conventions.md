# Fountain Scene Heading Conventions

## Scene Heading Structure

```
INT./EXT.   LOCATION   -   TIME OF DAY
```

Examples:
- `INT. KITCHEN - DAY`
- `EXT. ROOFTOP - NIGHT`
- `INT. CAR - MOVING`
- `EXT. FOREST - DAWN`

## Rules

- All caps.
- Always `INT.` (indoor) or `EXT.` (outdoor) or `INT./EXT.` (both — e.g. car window).
- Location is concise: `KITCHEN`, not `SARAH'S KITCHEN IN HER APARTMENT`.
- Time of DAY / NIGHT / DAWN / DUSK / CONTINUOUS / LATER / MOMENTS LATER.
- `CONTINUOUS` = follows immediately from previous scene.
- Blank line before and after every scene heading.

## Character Names
- All caps: `SARAH`
- First appearance: brief physical description in action line, not in character cue.
- `(V.O.)` = voice-over. `(O.S.)` = off-screen. `(CONT'D)` = speech continued after action.

## Transitions
- `CUT TO:` — immediate cut (use sparingly; cutting is implied).
- `SMASH CUT TO:` — abrupt jarring cut.
- `DISSOLVE TO:` — time passage.
- `FADE OUT.` — end of act or film.
- `FADE IN:` — opening of act or film.

## Action Lines
- Present tense only.
- Visual and auditory only — no internal thoughts.
- Short paragraphs (3–4 lines max). White space reads faster.

## Parentheticals
- Use sparingly — only when delivery is non-obvious.
- Wrong: `(says angrily)`. Right: `(quietly)` or `(to herself)`.
