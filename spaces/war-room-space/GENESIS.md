# GENESIS — War Room Space

## Why This Exists

Conflict is inevitable. Competitive threats, legal disputes, narrative attacks, and adversarial encounters require coordinated, full-spectrum response. War Room Space is the command center for strategic conflict management.

Without it: responses are reactive and uncoordinated, intelligence is scattered, and the narrative is lost to the opposition.

## What It Does

War Room Space manages conflict across all theaters simultaneously:
- **Intelligence operations** — gathering, logging, and analyzing adversary moves and patterns
- **Legal coordination** — tracking legal matters, filings, counsel notes, and status
- **Narrative warfare** — crafting and deploying messaging to counter attacks and control the frame
- **Strategic operations** — planning and executing strategic responses across theaters
- **Arsenal management** — cataloging available tools (cyber, legal, verbal, forensic, marketing)

## Core Agents

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| `strategist` | Command-level strategic planning and decision-making |
| `intelligence-officer` | Gathers and analyzes adversary intelligence |
| `legal-counsel` | Tracks legal exposure, filings, and defense options |
| `narrative-engineer` | Crafts counter-narratives and communications |

## Primary Formations

- **`ops-command`** — Active operation planning and execution
- **`intel-sweep`** — Intelligence gathering and analysis cycle
- **`narrative-war`** — Narrative defense and offense coordination

## Embedded Sub-Space

War Room contains an **incident-response** sub-space at `spaces/incident/` — a rapid-activation environment for acute crises requiring immediate coordinated response.

## Origin

Built for operators who understand that competitive, legal, or reputational threats require the same disciplined coordination as any other complex operation.
