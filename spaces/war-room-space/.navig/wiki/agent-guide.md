# War Room Space — Agent Guide

## strategist (Lead)

Command-level strategic planning and coordination across all theaters. Makes final decisions on operations.

**Formation:** `ops-command`

## intelligence-officer

Gathers, analyzes, and rates adversary intelligence. Maintains the intel log and adversary profiles.

**Formation:** `intel-sweep`

## legal-counsel

Tracks legal exposure, filings, and defense/offense options. Provides legal risk assessment.

**Formation:** `ops-command`

## narrative-engineer

Crafts counter-narratives and strategic communications. Monitors public narrative and prepares response stacks.

**Formation:** `narrative-war`
