# GENESIS — Homelab Space

## Why This Exists

A homelab is a living system — servers, NASes, switches, VMs, containers, and devices all running simultaneously. Homelab Space is the command center that keeps it all coherent.

Without it: hardware inventory is guesswork, network changes cause mystery outages, maintenance falls behind, and the lab becomes a liability instead of an asset.

## What It Does

Homelab Space manages every layer of the personal infrastructure stack:
- **Hardware inventory** — tracking every device, its specs, location, and health
- **Network operations** — scanning, port auditing, VLAN management, cable tracing
- **Service management** — Docker containers, VMs, self-hosted services
- **Maintenance** — updates, backups, disk health, GPU health, cleanup
- **Security** — SSH config, open port auditing, access control
- **Cross-platform ops** — Windows, Linux, and macOS unified management

## Origin

Built for homelab enthusiasts and self-hosters who want their personal infrastructure to be as well-managed as a professional data center.
