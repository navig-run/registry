## Common Protocols

| Protocol | Transport | Typical Devices | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **wifi** | TCP/IP over Wi-Fi | plugs, bulbs, cameras, ESP devices | Mains-powered; expected on the IoT VLAN |
| **zigbee** | 802.15.4 mesh via coordinator | sensors, buttons, bulbs | Reaches the bus through Zigbee2MQTT |
| **zwave** | Z-Wave mesh via controller | locks, sensors, dimmers | Bridged to MQTT via the controller |
| **matter** | Matter/Thread | new-generation devices | Interoperable across ecosystems |
| **ble** | Bluetooth Low Energy | trackers, low-rate sensors | Often gatewayed to MQTT |
