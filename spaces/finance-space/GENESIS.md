# Why Finance Space Exists

Most people have a vague sense of their finances. They know roughly how much comes in,
roughly how much goes out, and hope the direction is positive. That vagueness is expensive.

Finance Space exists to replace vague with precise.

Not through complexity — through a system that does the tracking automatically, surfaces
the numbers that matter, warns you before a month goes sideways, and replaces tax-season
panic with a folder that is always ready.

## Design Principles

**Numbers first, opinions second.**
Every recommendation is grounded in actual data. No generic advice. No "spend less on
coffee." Specific, actionable, context-aware.

**Monthly cadence is the atomic unit.**
Finances live and die on the monthly cycle. Every tool in this space is designed around
the close of a month: variance analysis, savings progress, investment review, tax carve.

**Separation of concerns.**
Budget tracking does not mix with investment tracking. Tax planning does not mix with
daily cash flow. Each domain has its own agent, its own data, and its own workflow.

**Private and local.**
All financial data stays on disk — no cloud sync, no third-party access. Credentials
for read-only API access (Open Banking, brokerage APIs) are stored in the NAVIG vault.

**Tax-year awareness.**
Every transaction is tagged to a tax year. Documents are organized by year. The tax
planner always knows exactly where you stand relative to the current year's filing.

## What This Space Is Not

It is not an accounting system for a business — use `business-space` for that.
It is not a crypto trading terminal — though investment-tracker handles portfolio
holdings including crypto.
It is not a financial advisor — it surfaces data and projections; decisions remain yours.
