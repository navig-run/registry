# Council Space — Context

## Purpose
Structured multi-agent debate and decision extraction. Each council session produces a decision card with rationale and dissents.

## Invoke When
- A decision requires multiple expert perspectives
- You want to stress-test a plan or hypothesis
- You want to surface blind spots in your thinking

## Council Composition Principles
- Include at least one agent that will argue against the default conclusion
- Balance domain expertise with contrarian thinking
- Odd number of agents to avoid tied votes
