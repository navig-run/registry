# GENESIS — Project Space

## Why This Space Exists

Personal projects die from three causes: unclear scope, invisible progress,
and unresolved blockers that sit untouched for weeks. The Project Space
addresses all three — making scope explicit, progress visible, and blockers
something that gets resolved the same day they're named.

## Design Principles

1. **Every project must have a clearly stated done-state.** A project without a
   definition of done is a hobby. The kickoff workflow enforces this.

2. **Milestones over tasks.** Milestones mark meaningful progress. Tasks exist
   to serve milestones, not the other way around.

3. **A blocker named is a blocker half-solved.** Blockers must be logged immediately
   and must have a next action. Stuck items with no defined next step are escalated.

4. **Weekly standup is non-negotiable.** Projects without a weekly review stall.
   The weekly-standup workflow touches every active project in under 10 minutes.

5. **Close projects properly.** Zombie projects — technically "open" but functionally
   abandoned — are the enemy. A project is either active, paused (with a resume date),
   or closed.

## Scope

This space covers: side projects, work projects you own end to end, home
improvement projects, learning-driven build projects, creative projects — any goal
that needs a plan.

It does NOT cover: company-wide portfolio and operations management with many
owners (→ company-space), routine recurring tasks (→ use a habit-tracking tool),
purely financial goals (→ finance-space).

## Core Cadences

- **On creation:** Kickoff — scope, milestones, blockers, done-state
- **Weekly:** Standup — update every project in <10 min
- **On completion:** Close — outcomes, what worked, what didn't
